TRANSPORT & RETURN

SpaceX

Falcon 9 rideshare

Shared launch manifests and documented payload interfaces provide a concrete integration reference. Our extension: schedule compatible manufacturing payloads around a booked mission.

Dragon research return

ISS research cargo return supplies an established logistics example for compatible hosted experiments. It is mission-specific integration, not pickup of arbitrary independent capsules.

Starfall

The June 2026 demonstration directly connects SpaceX to microgravity access and return. Cartridges and production software could sit above emerging infrastructure; routine service availability is not assumed.

Starship

A future capacity scenario consistent with its reusable transportation design. The first experiment and near-term business case should stand without a particular Starship schedule or price.

Connect a research request to a data loader, numerical optimizer or simulation. Developer-controlled tools validate and execute the requested operations.

Image understanding

Help an operator interpret experimental imagery and investigate potential anomalies, alongside calibrated vision measurements. Scientific accuracy must be evaluated.

The proposed integration

Paper → evidence record → candidate experiment → resource schedule → source-linked batch report. This presentation is an interactive concept, not a live Grok or spacecraft integration.
