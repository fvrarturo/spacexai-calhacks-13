Show the arithmetic and assumptions

Fixed mission cost F = 100 units; variable cost V = 20 per batch. Dedicated: (F + V) / q. Shared: (F / n + V) / q, where n is occupied compatible slots and q is accepted-batch yield.

The comparison assumes identical yield, no added coordination cost and sufficient power, thermal and return capacity. It excludes financing, regulation and additional infrastructure. It is not a quote, price estimate or forecast.
