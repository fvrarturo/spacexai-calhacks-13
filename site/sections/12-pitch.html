A 90-SECOND NARRATIVE

Some of the materials we need on Earth are limited by how they form. Gravity moves particles and fluids while crystals, pores and networks are taking shape.

Researchers have already used orbit to study cancer-drug formulations, pharmaceutical solid forms and ordered materials. But a successful experiment is only the beginning. Industry needs a process it can repeat.

Orbital Foundry is our proposal for that next layer: specialized manufacturing cartridges, a shared operating system and a network that connects laboratories on Earth to production capacity in space.

A lab brings a formulation and a target. We help select the experiment, schedule the process, observe its progress and connect the returned material to a complete batch record. SpaceX provides relevant routes to orbit and back; Grok can help organize the evidence and coordinate validated analytical tools.

At Cal Hacks, we make that future concrete with real NASA data and a transparent production-planning prototype. The ambition is a new industrial base above Earth—whose value is measured in better products and processes below.
