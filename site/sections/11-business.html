THE ECOSYSTEM IS ALREADY FORMING

Build a clear role within it.

Modular platforms and orbital manufacturing are already being pursued. Our proposed differentiation is cross-material recipe development, experiment/production scheduling, bounded control and evidence-linked batch qualification.

Space Cargo Unlimited / BentoBoxModular monitored payload platform; substantial overlap with shared services.

Redwire / SpaceMDAnnounced a 2028 Starfall mission with expected capacity for up to 32 PIL-BOX systems. A plan, not a completed mission.

G-SPACEExisting PSI image analytics and space/Earth comparison tools.

Space ForgeOrbital seeds followed by terrestrial scale-up; a useful architecture already under development.

Protect the customer’s IP. Separate private formulations and batch data. Share process knowledge only where authorized.

Earn the economic claim. Count integration, hardware, power, return, failures and qualification—not only launch cost per kilogram.

Start narrowly, design broadly. One cartridge family and a matched Earth baseline are enough to test the platform thesis.
