02.4 / DESIGN AROUND THE HARD PARTS

Space is the advantage.
Engineering is the work.

01 Heat has to leave. +

In vacuum, external heat rejection depends on radiation. We need stage-by-stage heat budgets, coupled thermal models and limits that prevent one hot process from disturbing a sensitive neighbor.

VERIFY / CALORIMETRY + THERMAL-VACUUM TEST
02 Quiet growth needs protection. +

Pumps, mixers and spacecraft maneuvers can disturb crystal growth. Measure the disturbance spectrum, isolate sources, and reserve quiet windows where the recipe needs them.

VERIFY / ACCELEROMETER DATA + CO-RUN EXPERIMENT
03 Shared services cannot mean shared contamination. +

Use sealed cartridges and distinct wetted paths. Verify leak containment, chemical compatibility, cleanliness, sterilization where needed and failure isolation for each material family.

VERIFY / LEAK TEST + CONTAMINATION ASSAY
04 The product has to survive the journey. +

Growth is only part of the mission. Stabilize samples, track thermal history and qualify vibration, re-entry and recovery exposure before comparing post-return performance.

VERIFY / RETURN ENVIRONMENT + FUNCTIONAL RETEST
05 Autonomy needs a safe boundary. +

A validated controller enforces operating limits and fault responses. Research assistants can propose experiments and summarize evidence, while command execution stays in explicit, tested control logic.

VERIFY / HARDWARE-IN-THE-LOOP + FAULT INJECTION

02.7 / BIG DATA, NEXT

A foundry
that measures itself.

Every foundry will be an instrument as well as a factory. These data streams turn each flight into a better design for the next one.

01 / ACCELERATION

Measure our own microgravity.

Fly an accelerometer with every cartridge and compare it with tracking-derived drag and published ISS spectra. Quiet windows become a schedulable resource.

02 / THERMAL & POWER

Close the loop on heat.

Log stage-level power and radiator temperatures, then fit coupled thermal models that decide which processes can run side by side.

03 / EDGE

Measure in orbit, send down answers.

Run validated crystal-metrology models on board. Downlink features and flagged frames; keep full image sets for the batches that matter.

04 / SAFETY

Share our ephemeris.

Publish precise orbit data to operator platforms and screen conjunctions continuously. A crowded shell is a shared responsibility.
