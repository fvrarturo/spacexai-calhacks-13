00.1 / THE PROBLEM

Gravity is part
of the recipe.

On Earth, particles settle. Warm fluid rises. These familiar forces can also disrupt the structures we’re trying to make.

A DIFFERENT PROCESS ENVIRONMENTMECHANISM SKETCH

1 g On Earth

Settling & buoyancy-driven flow

μg In orbit

Less settling. A new growth regime.

Schematic motion, not simulated physics. Surface forces, diffusion, residual acceleration and process conditions still matter.

01

Structure shapes performance.

Crystal size, phase and defects can affect how a drug suspension flows, a membrane separates ions, or a photonic structure handles light.

02

Orbit changes the conditions.

Free fall suppresses sedimentation and buoyancy-driven convection. Some processes can access structures that are difficult to reproduce on Earth.

03

A beautiful crystal isn’t enough.

The material must outperform a strong Earth control, survive return, and justify the cost for a specific application.
