## How this was gathered

Web pages, agency reports, company posts, Chinese press, conference PDFs, and papers, fetched on 24 October 2026. Every factual claim in these notes is tied to a link. Industry-insider numbers that are not official statistics are marked as claims.

X was requested through this account and did not work:

- The X search tool returned “X search is not available for this account.”
- Chrome is open on this Mac, but driving it needs Computer Use (Accessibility and Screen Recording). That permission was not granted, so the logged-in session was not read.
- The in-editor browser opened `x.com` and was sent to a login wall. That browser is not your account.

Posts that reporters and X mirrors quoted from SpaceX and from @SawyerMerritt are included in [03-voices-nasa-esa-spacex.md](03-voices-nasa-esa-spacex.md), with the quoting source named. A logged-in pass through NASA, ESA, and Chinese space accounts is the main hole left in the report.
