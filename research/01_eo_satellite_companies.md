# Commercial Earth-Observation Satellite Operators and Startups (ex-China): Problems, Responses, Data, and Hackathon Angles

**Scope:** commercial EO satellite operators and startups outside China, grouped by sensing type (optical/VHR, SAR, hyperspectral, thermal, RF geolocation, GHG/methane, weather/RO/microwave, niche), plus cross-cutting operator problems (ground stations, rideshare dependence, replenishment, calibration/interoperability, licensing, shutter control, sanctions, government dependence).

Researched on 2026-10-24

**How to read:** sections A–G are one entry per company grouped by sensing type; section H covers niche platforms; section I covers cross-cutting operator problems (X1–X14); then the Problem bank (numbered), Cross-slice leads and Leads not followed. Each entry uses the fields What it is / Problems / How they're solving it / Data / Hackathon angle / Credibility / Sources (inline links with dates). Items not confirmed on an opened page are marked *unverified* or *(not opened)*.

**Research limits:** the shared web-search budget ran out repeatedly during this pass, so most facts come from directly opened primary pages (company newsrooms, SEC filings, job boards, Wikipedia, trade press). Reddit was not reachable; Hacker News was used via its public Algolia index.

### A5. Albedo (VLEO 10 cm visible + thermal)

- **What it is:** US startup flying satellites in very low Earth orbit (~275 km) to reach 10 cm-class visible and thermal imagery; contracted by NRO (SCE EO roster), Space Force, AFRL and NASA JPL. [Albedo homepage](https://albedo.com/); [Breaking Defense, 4 Dec 2025](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/12/nro-extends-contracts-for-4-providers-readies-new-acquisition-approach/)
- **Clarity-1 post-mortem (published 23 Jan 2026):** launched 14 Mar 2025 on Transporter-13. *Worked:* drag coefficient 12% better than design ("VLEO works. And it works better than even we expected"), ~5-year life at 275 km averaged over the solar cycle, atomic-oxygen-resilient arrays, automated contact planning across **25 ground stations**, 30+ maneuvers/day, X-band 800 Mbps, processed snippets in Slack ~30 s after downlink, microbolometer thermal imagery (ships in Tokyo Bay, coke ovens). *Failed:* one of four control-moment gyros failed on 14 Apr 2025 (lubricant's real temperature limit lower than spec), a second showed the same signs; torque-rod-only control gave 15–20° (sometimes ~45°) attitude error and 50+ pixels of smear; "reached 10 cm imagery 98% of the way" but did not fully achieve it; **imagery is uncalibrated**; focus drift from hygroscopic dry-out; comms lost ~9 months in, suspected TT&C radio memory corruption. Lessons: cooler CMGs, stiffer secondary-mirror structure, in-house TT&C radio. [Albedo blog, 23 Jan 2026](https://albedo.com/post/clarity-1-what-worked-and-where-we-go-next)
- **Next:** Vicinity-1 (launch 2027) — higher-power VLEO bus with deployable arrays for SAR, EW, direct-to-device comms; argues VLEO is below the Van Allen belts and debris decays faster. [Albedo blog, 7 Apr 2026](https://albedo.com/post/albedos-next-vleo-mission-vicinity)
- **Problems surfaced:** pointing/jitter and smear at 10 cm; calibration of imagery; single-point-of-failure components (CMGs, third-party radios); downlink capacity limited imagery collection.
- **Hackathon angle:** super-resolution / deblurring of smeared imagery is a classic CV problem; motion-blur estimation from satellite attitude telemetry is too deep for 36 h, but a "how much does resolution matter for task X" demo using Maxar Open Data downsampled to 30/50/100 cm is doable.
- **Credibility:** official company blog (candid post-mortem).

- **Credibility:** official vendor blog.

### X6. US licensing regime (NOAA/Commerce CRSRA)

- CRSRA (Office of Space Commerce) licenses private remote-sensing systems under 51 U.S.C. § 60101 et seq. and 15 C.F.R. 960; tiering under 15 CFR 960.6: **Tier 1** = unenhanced data substantially the same as available from unlicensed (e.g., foreign) sources; **Tier 2** = same as available only from licensed US entities; **Tier 3** = not substantially available anywhere → most conditions; systems can be moved to a lower tier as foreign capability advances (operators submit evidence via a Data Availability Notification Form). Completeness review ≤7 days; license processing up to 60 days. July 2024 listening session; RFI on satellite disposal requirements. [CRSRA](https://www.space.commerce.gov/regulations/commercial-remote-sensing-regulatory-affairs/); [Tier categorization](https://www.space.commerce.gov/regulations/commercial-remote-sensing-regulatory-affairs/tier-categorization/); [Licensing](https://www.space.commerce.gov/regulations/commercial-remote-sensing-regulatory-affairs/licensing/)
- **Licensing and compliance friction seen elsewhere:** HawkEye 360 holds "over 200 export licenses" and warns violations could mean "loss of our ability to export products or perform services" [S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1750704/000162828026024593/hawkeye360-sx1.htm); Planet is hiring a "Technical Program Manager, Trade Compliance" and two "EU Regulatory Licensing & Spectrum Manager" roles [Planet jobs](https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/planetlabs/jobs); ground stations must be "licensed for the constellation" (Capella) [Capella](https://www.capellaspace.com/resources/optimizing-the-entire-path-from-orbit-to-delivery); "ITAR-free" is now a sales feature for European competitors (X7). The 1997 Kyl–Bingaman Amendment (Israel resolution cap) was a licensing-era restriction lifted in 2020 and resurfaced on HN in Mar 2026. [HN item](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47372406); [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/world/access-to-satellite-imagery-restricted-amid-iran-war-report/3866406)
- **Hackathon angle:** "foreign availability tracker" — a public catalogue of what resolution/revisit/sensing type is available from non-US sources (ESA, Airbus, ICEYE, Chinese Jilin-1, Indian Pixxel…) so a US operator can argue for Tier 1. Small but concrete regulatory-tech idea.
