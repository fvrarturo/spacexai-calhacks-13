# Research index (all files)

This is research for the SpaceXSI "Make it Legendary" challenge at Cal Hacks 13.0, gathered on 24 Oct 2026. Two research passes ran in parallel and both are kept in this folder:

- **Underscore files (`00_` … `12_`)**: an exhaustive pass. Ten research agents covered the web and a separate pass read X/Twitter in a logged-in browser. Every claim is linked and tagged for credibility.
- **Hyphen files (`00-` … `08-`)**: a shorter, curated pass, indexed in [README.md](README.md). Its X access failed, and [11_x_twitter_findings.md](11_x_twitter_findings.md) fills that gap.

**Start here:** [12_idea_bank.md](12_idea_bank.md) has the ranked ideas, then [08-ideas.md](08-ideas.md) has the parallel shortlist. Go to a topic file only when you need the evidence behind an idea.

## Caveats on the research
- The web-search quota shared by the parallel agents ran out several times, so most facts come from primary pages that were opened directly. Each file's "Leads not followed" section lists what could not be checked.
- Reddit could not be reached, and some sites blocked fetching (Reuters, SpaceNews, Nature, BBC, some conference sites). Those items come from secondary sources and are marked.
- X findings come from reading search results and timelines; engagement counts are as of 24 Oct 2026. Low-credibility viral posts are kept but tagged.
- Anything marked "unverified" or "rumor" should be checked before it goes on a slide.
